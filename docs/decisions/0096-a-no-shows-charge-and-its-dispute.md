# 0096. A no-show's charge and its dispute, and the free change a visit ops moved keeps

- Status: accepted, on the owner's rulings of 27 September 2026 (`docs/open-points.md`, items 60 and 71); amended 30 September 2026 on the owner's ruling (ADR 0025, item 85): a charge may be disputed for 30 days after it, a console setting kept on each charge as its deadline (migration 0065); amended 1 October 2026 by [0105](0105-a-consultation-and-fit-in-one-visit.md): a consultation and fit in one visit, which holds no payment, is sold to cost nothing as a no-show or moved late
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Amends [0074](0074-hand-offs-and-messages.md), under which a charge kept what the visit took and nothing recorded an amount; amends [0046](0046-moving-and-cancelling.md), whose notice counted from the visit's own time however it came to be there; amends [0088](0088-every-policy-in-the-console.md), whose no-show charge nothing applied yet and whose move panel named a fixed 24 hours; records three departures in ADR 0025 (items 85 to 87)

## Context

**The charge.** The owner ruled on 27 September 2026 (`docs/owner-answers-2026-09-27.md`, item 60): "a charged no-show costs, to begin with, what a late cancellation of the same visit costs (first fit ₹4,000, replacement ₹3,000, a paid service visit kept, a credit lost), set in the console apart from the late-cancellation terms so either can change alone; the client disputes a charge in the app, and ops rule Refund or Uphold in the console with a reason, and the client is told." ADR 0088 built the setting, `no_show_charge`, and kept it on each hold as sold, but nothing charged by it: a charge kept whatever the visit took (ADR 0074), nothing recorded an amount, so board D1's "Charges and no-shows" figure left the no-shows out and counted them beside it ("1 no-show not charged yet"), and the board's disputed charge was a line saying no client could raise one. `GET /api/no-shows` gave the distance a check-in measured and not the radius it was measured against, which the board letters beside it ("240 m · over 200 m fence"; item 56).

**The free change.** Item 71: ops may move a visit to a time inside the client's notice, and the client's own cancel or move of it afterwards then counted as late, keeping the late fee or losing the credit. The owner ruled that "the client keeps the free change": "after a move by ops, the client's notice counts from the visit's time before ops moved it."

## Decision

### A charge costs what the booking was sold to cost a no-show

When ops charge a no-show (`POST /api/no-shows/{id}/decision`), the charge is the no-show charge the booking was sold under: the one its hold kept, the committed one where the hold kept none, or, for a visit ops booked in FSM that no hold sold, the one in force (`termsOfVisit`, `src/domain/visit-changes.ts`, which a client's change reads too). What it costs is what a cancel inside the notice of the same visit would (`chargedRefund` and `chargedCredit`, `src/policy/no-show.ts`, quoting the owner):

| Charge   | The visit's payment                                                                              | A credit it was paid with                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Late fee | The late fee the booking was sold under, or its kind's on its day, is kept; the rest is refunded | (a credit pays for a service visit, which has no late fee) |
| Visit    | Kept                                                                                             | Spent                                                      |
| Nothing  | Refunded                                                                                         | Returned                                                   |

- **The ruling records it**: `no_show_cases.charge`, `kept_amount` and `refund_amount` (migration 0059), in the ruling's batch with its audit entry, the client's message and a credit returned. The refund of the rest goes to Razorpay after the batch, at most what is left of the payment; one Razorpay refuses is left to ops, told once with the visit and the payment, as a cancel's is.
- **A credit given back comes back only to a grant that can still take it**, as a cancel's does: one expired or clawed back since the visit cannot. The client's message then says so, composed from what the ledger holds and not from what the ruling meant to give (`no_show_waived_credit_gone_v1`, `no_show_dispute_credit_gone_v1`, as `visit_cancelled_credit_gone_v1` for a cancel), and ops are told once (`no_show_credit_not_back:<why>:<visit>`) to settle with the client by hand whether they are owed one. The same holds of a waiver that returns the credit.
- **A ruling that meets another writes nothing.** The ruling's update is the claim: it writes the request's own ruling ID (`ruling_id`, migration 0059) only where no ruling is yet, and everything in its batch beside it, the audit entry, the client's message and a credit returned, is written only where the case carries that ID (`src/domain/ruling-claims.ts`). Where another member of staff ruled first, the route answers not found, refunds nothing, and neither tells the client nor records a ruling it did not make. One batch, so the ruling and all it writes happen, or none of them does.
- **Nothing stands between a committed ruling and its refund** (`afterRuling`, `src/domain/after-a-ruling.ts`). A retry of a committed ruling answers not found, and the client's message already says the money is on its way, so a failure after the commit must reach ops: the refund reads the payment inside the step that alerts ops when it fails, so a lost read raises `no_show_refund_failed` with the amount owed, as Razorpay's refusal does; the client's message is queued next; and whether a credit given back came back is read last.
- **A visit of no kind we sell** keeps what it took, as every charge did before; none is known to exist.
- **A case charged before this release** recorded no amount. It keeps its ruling, reads as charged, and cannot be disputed, since nothing says what it took; board D1 lists it with "Amount not recorded". Staging alone holds any.

### Board D1's money holds the no-shows

`GET /api/payments` adds what each charged no-show kept to `charged`, "Charges and no-shows", and lists each with its amount. `no_shows_charged` and the always-null `dispute` are gone from the answer: the figure no longer leaves anything out, and a dispute belongs to no day. As a late cancellation that kept nothing is not listed, a no-show whose charge kept no money is not either: a credit it spent is in the client's credits, not the day's money. A refund a dispute gives is a refund on its own day, among "Refunds processing"; the charge stays what was charged on its day.

### The client disputes the charge in the app, and ops refund or uphold it

- **The client** (`POST /api/visits/{id}/dispute`, `{ reason }`, at most 300 characters): on a visit of theirs whose latest case ops charged, once a charge (`no_show_disputes.case_id` is unique), and only of a charge that took something, money it kept or a credit it spent (`isDisputable`). Refused with `409 already_disputed` or `409 not_disputable`, `404` where there is no charged no-show of theirs. Ops are alerted, naming the visit and neither the client nor their words; the audit entry (`no_show.dispute`) names the dispute. Not behind `SELF_SERVE_BOOKING`: any charge can be disputed. No time limit: the owner set none.
- **The app** says on the visit's page what the charge took ("Charged: we kept Rs. 4,000 of what you paid.", or that the credit is spent), where a dispute stands (open, refunded, upheld; after a refund, only that, since the charge no longer stands), and, where the charge can be disputed, "Dispute this charge", which opens a sheet in the note sheet's frame for the client's reason. `GET /api/visits/{id}` and the payments' `no_show` answer `charge`, `dispute` and `disputable`. A credit that came back, by a charge of nothing or a refunded dispute, reads in Payments as used and returned, never lost.
- **Ops** (`GET /api/no-shows/disputes`, `POST /api/no-shows/disputes/{id}/ruling`): board D1's second card, one a dispute, above the queue, with the board's label, the client's words, what the charge took, the board's four rows of evidence, the required note and Refund and Uphold. Each ruling needs its reason (`needsReason("no_show_dispute", …)`), kept on the dispute under who ruled, and audited with the ruling's code alone (`no_show.dispute_rule`). Ruled once, as a case is, and a ruling that meets another writes nothing, as a case's does (`no_show_disputes.ruling_id`).
- **Refund gives back what the charge took**: the amount it kept, refunded after the batch, and the credit it spent, back in its grant in the batch, as a waiver's is. It does so whatever ops have set a waiver to give back (item 87 of ADR 0025): a ruling named Refund that kept the money would not be one.
- **Uphold keeps it.**
- **The client is told either way** by the message path ADR 0074 uses, `no_show_dispute_ruled`, with their consent to WhatsApp about visits, and never ops' reason: refunded with what goes back and where, or the credit back, or that it could not come back; upheld, that the charge stands, and that they may message us to ask why, a problem being what "Message us" is for (ADR 0025, item 70).
- **Their words and ops'** are on the dispute alone, never in the audit log (ADR 0031). An erasure blanks both, the client's with their own notes and ops' with their other decisions' reasons; the ruling stays. The client's export carries each dispute, in their words, with the ruling and when, as it carries their grievances; not ops' reason, as it carries no ruling's reason.

### The radius in force, beside the distance

Each case on `GET /api/no-shows`, and each dispute, carries `radius_m`, the radius the check-in kept as in force when it measured (ADR 0061), not the one ops have set since. The console letters the board's "240 m · over 200 m fence", over it in the oxblood the board draws, and inside it "12 m · inside 200 m fence" (a placeholder).

### What the client is told of a charge

The texts no longer say "As with a late cancel", which stopped being true once the charge was set apart from the late-cancel terms (ADR 0088). Each says what the charge kept, what goes back and where, or that the credit is spent, and that a client who was home can dispute it in the app, where "Message us if this is wrong" stood (`no_show_charged_paid_v1`, `no_show_charged_fee_v1`, `no_show_charged_credit_v1`). A charge of nothing reads as a waiver that gives both back. The words are placeholders, for the Word file of `docs/open-points.md`, item 39, with the dispute's four (`no_show_dispute_refunded_v1`, `no_show_dispute_credit_back_v1`, `no_show_dispute_credit_gone_v1`, `no_show_dispute_upheld_v1`) and `no_show_waived_credit_gone_v1`.

### After ops move a visit, the client counts from its time before

- **Which time.** `appointments.start_before_move` keeps the time the client last chose: set to the visit's time by the first move ops make after it, and kept through every later one. The ruling says "the visit's time before ops moved it"; after two moves by ops that is the time the client chose, not one ops chose for them, so the free change they keep is the one they had.
- **Counted from the later of the two.** A client's own cancel or move counts its notice from the visit's window as it was before the move, or as it is now where that is later (`noticeCountsFrom`, `src/policy/moving-a-visit.ts`). A move later only gives the client more notice; counted from the earlier time, it would take a free change away, which the ruling means never to do (item 86 of ADR 0025).
- **Which moves.** Every move on the dispatch board that changes the time, and a time FSM holds that the mirror did not write, which is a move made in FSM itself, since our own moves write the mirror as they write FSM. A move ops make because the client asked (`client_asked`) is the client's own choice of time, as a move in the app is, and clears it (item 86 of ADR 0025). So does the client's own move in the app; a charged move books a new visit, which starts with none.
- **The boundary**: a visit booked for Thursday that ops move to Tuesday morning, 21 hours away, is free to cancel at once (`test/worker/visit-changes.test.ts`). The note FSM keeps says so: "more than 24 hours before the time ops moved it from".
- **The move panel** names the notice the visit was sold under, from the board (`notice_hours` on each block), where it named a fixed 24 hours: "This visit is inside 12 hours. The client is not charged, because we moved it."

## Consequences

- Migration 0059: `no_show_cases.charge`, `kept_amount`, `refund_amount`, `ruling_id`; the table `no_show_disputes`, with an index of the open ones and one by person; `appointments.start_before_move`. Every column is nullable and new: the Worker already deployed reads none of them, and a no-show it charges, or a move it makes, reads as one ruled or moved before.
- The contract: `GET /api/no-shows` answers `radius_m`; `GET /api/payments` drops `no_shows_charged` and `dispute`; `GET /api/visits/{id}` and the client's payments answer `charge`, `dispute` and `disputable` on `no_show`; three routes, `POST /api/visits/{id}/dispute`, `GET /api/no-shows/disputes`, `POST /api/no-shows/disputes/{id}/ruling`; the error codes `already_disputed` and `not_disputable`; the dispatch board's blocks answer `notice_hours`.
- Settings · Rules' note on `no_show_charge` says what a charge costs, where it said the charge was not in force.
- The go-live proof of the late fee (`docs/go-live.md`, step 8) moves the fit itself inside the notice, since ops moving it there no longer makes the client's move late.
- An open dispute waits on the Tasks board as a Disputed charge, with its own allowance (`no_show_dispute`, two days to begin with), and its task links to its row on No-shows; ops are alerted as one is raised too.
- Tests: `test/worker/no-show-disputes.test.ts`, and additions to `ops-no-shows`, `ops-payments`, `owed-messages`, `client-payments`, `client-visits`, `visit-changes` and `dispatch`; `test/node/policy-no-show`, `policy-moving-a-visit`, `policy-dispatch`, `policy-decision-reasons` and `ops-settings`; `e2e/ops/no-shows.e2e.ts` and `e2e/app/no-show-dispute.e2e.ts`, each with axe.
