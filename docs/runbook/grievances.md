# Grievances

The terms promise every complaint an acknowledgement within 48 hours and a resolution within 30 days (clause 13.6). The Grievance Officer is Adwate Kumar, Co-founder and COO, at grievance@maneman.in and +91 90079 73247, as the privacy policy and the terms name him.

## A concern raised in the app

The app says "Received" the moment it is sent, and WhatsApp says so too (`concern_received_v1`). The console lists it under Grievances, with the 30 days counting down, and the Tasks board holds it until it is answered. Answer it there: the client reads the answer in the app, under Profile, and WhatsApp tells them it is there (`concern_answered_v1`).

## A complaint by email, post or phone

Nothing in the console sees these, so the Grievance Officer keeps them:

1. **Within 48 hours:** reply from grievance@maneman.in that it has arrived, and that we will resolve it within 30 days of receiving it.
2. **Note the date it arrived.** The 30 days run from then.
3. **Resolve it within the 30 days,** in writing, by the way it came. If it is about the client's data, the console's client page has what we hold (Download their data).
4. **Keep the thread** in the grievance@ mailbox: it is the record.

A complaint that is a personal data breach is [A personal data breach](data-breach.md) first.
