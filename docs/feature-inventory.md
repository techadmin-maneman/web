## Try-on (`/try`)

F3 wired the try-on to the API. The photograph is prepared in the browser and uploads while the visitor chooses. Since the owner's ruling of 1 October 2026 the look goes to WhatsApp only, never to the site (ADR 0104): the gate follows the looks and needs the name and number, the claim saves them before the look is asked for, and the sent screen says it is on its way. There is no countdown and no result screen (ADR 0022, 18–25).
