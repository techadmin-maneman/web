# Open points before production

Staging runs on placeholders wherever an input is still owed (ADR 0025, item 27). Every placeholder is listed here, with every other point that must be settled before Phase 2 reaches production. Nothing goes to production while an item here is open. When one is settled, record the answer, the date and where it now lives, and move it to "Settled" at the end.

How to hand over each answer is in `docs/phase2-inputs.md`.

With all three the same call answers 201. A closed job is now invoiced by the five-minute pass and nobody has to remember.
  - **Finding it.** No listing is needed. The work order's **service line** carries `Invoice_Id` — it is the work order's own top-level fields that do not, which is what the proof saw — and that invoice's `ZBilling_InvoiceId` is the Books ID `books.invoicePdf` wants. It is read the same way whoever raised it, so an invoice the owner raises by hand in FSM's screen is picked up within five minutes.
  - What was left, the invoice being a draft, is settled below (item 70).
- **A visit's invoice is issued as it is raised (item 70).** Ruled by the owner on 23 September 2026: the invoice is marked sent in Books as part of raising it, so it is a valid tax invoice, and only then may the client see it (`docs/decisions/0056-issuing-the-invoice.md`). The CA's view on GST is still wanted (items 2 and 3), but it does not hold the sending back. Two things follow.
  - **No draft that already exists is ever sent by code**, including one an earlier pass raised and could not send. A draft can be deleted and an issued invoice only voided with a credit note, so the backlog's drafts in the owner's real Books stay the owner's to delete (item 10). **Deal with them by hand before this is deployed.**
  - **A client's advance now settles against their invoice.** ADR 0044's payments pass waits only while an invoice is a draft, so an issued one is paid down on the next five-minute run with no further change.
- **Production's Access service token (item 30).** `mm-ci-production` has a Service Auth policy on production's Access applications (ops, app, tech), set up by the owner on 22 September 2026. It is kept in `.env.production-access` and as the `production` environment's `CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_ID` and `CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_SECRET` in GitHub; `verify-ci-secrets` checks it against the ops console (`docs/runbook.md`, step 3).
- **How long a refund takes (item 36).** The app says "5 to 7 working days", Razorpay's time for a normal refund. Ruled 22 September 2026 (ADR 0025, item 28).
- **The UPI app on a payment (item 37).** The app shows "UPI" alone. Ruled 22 September 2026 (ADR 0025, item 29).
- **An invited friend is in the CRM (item 50).** Settled 24 September 2026 by making `leads.loss_extent` optional, so every booking leaves a lead whichever page it came from (`docs/decisions/0060-an-invited-friend-reaches-ops-and-the-crm.md`, migration 0031). The landing was not made to ask: it is deliberately short, and a friend arriving on someone else's invite is the visitor a question costs most. Migration 0025 could not do this because it rebuilt the table and `tryon_jobs` points at a lead; 0031 swaps the column in place instead, so `leads` is never dropped. **The trade:** an invited friend's lead carries no loss extent, and Zoho leaves the field empty rather than guessing.

They write the person, the consent, the invite's attribution, the lead and a row in `consultation_requests`, hold no slot, and answer `201` with `state: "requested"`; the page says ops will fix the hour on WhatsApp. Ops see it in the console's task queue, and it leaves that queue when the client has a consultation (`docs/decisions/0060-an-invited-friend-reaches-ops-and-the-crm.md`, migration 0032). Production no longer has to keep self-serve on for invites.
