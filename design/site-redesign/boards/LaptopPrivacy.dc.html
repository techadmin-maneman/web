Welcome to Mane Man's privacy policy (“Privacy Policy”).

Mane Man Private Limited and its affiliates (collectively, “Mane Man”, “we” or “us”) are engaged in the business of fitting and servicing non-surgical hair systems for our customers at their homes or otherwise. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices in relation to the collection, storage, usage, processing, and disclosure of personal data that you have consented to share with us when you access, use, or otherwise interact with our website available at https://maneman.in and our app available at https://app.maneman.in (together, the “Platform”) or avail products or services that Mane Man offers you on or through the Platform (collectively, the “Services”).

At Mane Man, we are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. In order to provide you with access to the Services, we have to collect and otherwise process certain data about you. This Privacy Policy explains how we process and use personal data about you.

Please note that unless specifically defined in this Privacy Policy, capitalised terms shall have the same meaning ascribed to them in our Terms and Conditions, available at https://maneman.in/terms (“Terms”). Please read this Privacy Policy in consonance with the Terms.

By using the Services, you confirm that you have read and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy and consent to the processing activities described under this Privacy Policy.

Please refer to Section 1 to understand how the terms of this Privacy Policy apply to you.
