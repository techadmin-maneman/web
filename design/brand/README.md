# Mane Man — logo files

Thirteen SVGs. Every file is a filled outline: no strokes to expand, no live
text, no embedded fonts. One path per colour, so each file prints in a single
ink.

## The mark

    mark-navy.svg      #16233A   on paper
    mark-gilt.svg      #C9A363   on ink — the primary pairing
    mark-white.svg     #FFFFFF   knockout
    mark-black.svg     #000000   single-colour print, stamps, fax-grade repro

Artboard 200 × 217.4. Minimum reproduction **9 mm / 34 px wide**. Below that,
use the favicon drawing — the fan strands close up and the crooks fill in.

## The wordmark

    wordmark-navy.svg         display cut, on paper
    wordmark-white.svg        display cut, on ink
    wordmark-small-navy.svg   small cut, on paper
    wordmark-small-white.svg  small cut, on ink

Two cuts, not one scaled. The display cut has 3.4:1 stroke contrast and is for
**112 px / 30 mm and above**. Below that its hairlines drop under 1 px and the
M loses its left stem — use the small cut, which is drawn wider with heavier
hairlines and shallower serifs. The two are not interchangeable at the same
size; pick by final width, not by preference.

Letterforms are drawn, not set. Do not attempt to reproduce them by typing
EB Garamond and letter-spacing it.

## The lockup

    lockup-navy.svg    both parts navy
    lockup-gilt.svg    gilt mark, paper wordmark — for ink grounds
    lockup-white.svg   full knockout

Artboard 137.5 × 40. Mark 40 tall, wordmark cap height 14, optically centred.
The gap between them is one third of the mark's width and is **structural** —
do not close it, and keep at least that much clear space on all four sides.
Minimum reproduction **26 mm / 92 px wide**.

## Favicons

    favicon-32.svg     crown with the crooks legible
    favicon-16.svg     outer silhouette only

Neither is the mark scaled down. At 16 px the interior line work turns to mud,
so that cut carries the silhouette alone. Export to PNG at 16, 32, 48 and 180
(Apple touch icon) against `#16233A`.

## Never

Stretch, condense or rotate. Add a shadow, bevel, gradient or outline.
Recolour outside the four cuts above. Place the mark on a gold field — gold is
the mark's own colour and the field cancels it. Set the wordmark in a typeface.
Use the display cut below 112 px.

Gold is an accent: the mark, and one rule. It is never a fill and never body
text.
