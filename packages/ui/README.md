# @maneman/ui

The React parts the three Phase 2 apps share: the client app, the ops console and the technician app. What they have in common is written here once, so a fix reaches all three (ADR 0076). The calls to the API, India's dates, rupees and WhatsApp's links are `@maneman/web-kit`'s. The public site is Astro and Preact; its islands draw every control from here, through Preact's compatibility layer (ADR 0121), and its Astro pages give their links and buttons the looks exported here.

## Patterns

What a new screen uses for what it has to say, so every screen says it alike.

### A screen's four states

- **Loading:** `Loading` from `@maneman/ui/States`, with the words a screen reader hears; never a spinner. A page read again keeps what it shows (`useLiveLoad`'s `refresh`).
- **Failed:** `Failed`, one line and "Try again", where the page's data did not come; `ErrorRef` beside it carries the request's ID wherever the API answered. A write that did not go through says so beside its button, which stays (`InlineMessage` with the `error` tone).
- **Asked first:** a tap that cannot be taken back asks before it goes: `ConfirmSheet` in the technician app and the console, and the card's own question, in its own words, in the client app.
- **Done:** `InlineMessage` with the `saved` tone where the change was made, or the next step itself.

Every write runs through `useOneAtATime`, so a second tap inside the first's round trip asks nothing (ADR 0058); ESLint refuses a hand-set busy flag in the client and technician apps.

### Saving, deleting, and saying how things stand

- **A change of a value:** in place, on its own row (`EditableRow`, ADR 0212): Enter or Save sends it, and "Saved" shows beside it. A price asks its day in the same row. A change of many boxes at once, as a new record, is a form with its own Save.
- **Deleting:** a row's `Delete` asks once, then the `destructive` button deletes for good (the console's `DeleteAction`). A removal that can be made again, such as rejecting a plan line, is `danger`; one that cannot, deleting, erasing or refunding, is `destructive`.
- **Nothing to show:** `EmptyState`, the page's two lines in the client app or one muted line in a console panel, with a way on where there is one.
- **An error:** a page that did not load is `Failed` with "Try again" and its `ErrorRef`; a write that did not go through is an `InlineMessage` in the `error` tone beside its button, in the words of the refusal (`@maneman/web-kit/refusal`).
- **A status in a row or on a card:** `badgeLook()` from `@maneman/ui/Caps`, in the brass, muted or ink tone, and the status roles for a figure's colour: `--status-error` for what went wrong, `--status-warning` for what to act on (overdue, late, low), `--status-attention` for what to look at, `--status-done` for what is finished.
- **A chosen option:** `ToggleButton` or `ChoiceCard`, whose one chosen look is the package's; a screen never draws its own.

## Sizes by app

A button's size is what it is for, and each app says how big that is. `base.css` sets the client app's (`--action-height` 56 px at 16, `--control-height` 48 at 15, `--small-height` 44 at 15); the console sets a desk's, 40, 36 and 32 at 14, its boxes 36 (`apps/ops/src/styles/global.css`); the technician app sets its own on its body, from its board's "Targets": 64 at 18, 56 at 17 and 48 at 17, its outlined words in the regular weight.

## Rules

- **Tokens only.** Every colour, size and space comes from `@maneman/brand`, as in the apps; `test/node/apps/app/app-tokens.test.ts` reads these stylesheets too. A paper look draws in the `--ui-*` roles (`packages/brand/tokens.css`), which the console's dark theme turns to the ink's; an ink look draws in the ink's own colours.
- **A screen's own class wins.** `base.css` is the `base` cascade layer and each component's stylesheet the `ui` layer, above it; an app's own rules are in no layer, so they beat both. A screen sets a shared component's margin or width with its own class, and nothing else is needed. It never sets a button's ground, words' colour or edge, which would erase its pressed, disabled and busy looks; `test/dom/ui-button-looks.test.ts` reads every class an app hands a button. Every stylesheet here names the two layers first (`@layer base, ui;`), so the order holds whichever a browser meets first. The visually-hidden recipe alone stays outside the layers, so no screen can bring its words into view.
- **Each app brings its own React.** This package imports `react` but does not install it. The site's islands get Preact's compatibility layer in its place, so what the site uses holds to what Preact has; the site's tsconfig types `react` as `preact/compat` too, so an island hands a shared part Preact's children. ESLint lints this package by its own tsconfig, before the site's. Each app's `vite.config.ts` dedupes `react` and `react-dom`, since from here Node would find the repository root's React 18, kept for the fidelity runs (`docs/fidelity-method.md`).
- **Every control is drawn here.** No app or site island draws its own button, link, field, choice or fold; an Astro page's raw element takes `buttonLook()`, `textLinkLook()`, `iconButtonLook()` or the ruled disclosure's classes. A look the apps lack is added here as a variant, never drawn on a screen. `test/node/architecture/shared-controls.test.ts` holds it.
- **No copies in the apps.** `test/node/front-ends/ui-package.test.ts` fails an app that keeps its own icon, mark, router, loader, tap guard or error boundary, hides words its own way, or calls `/api/` itself.
- **Sources, not builds,** as `@maneman/brand`: each app's Vite compiles what it imports. `packages/ui/tsconfig.json` type-checks and lints the package on its own.
